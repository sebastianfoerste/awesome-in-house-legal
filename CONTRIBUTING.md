# Contributing

Suggestions are welcome. This list is curated, so every entry is reviewed before it goes in.

## What qualifies

- Open source, with a public repository and a recognised license.
- Genuinely useful to an in-house legal team: legal ops, contracts, compliance, privacy, legal AI, or legal data. General-purpose tools qualify only when the legal use case is concrete.
- Alive. Archived projects are accepted only when they remain a reference point for the field, and they are flagged as archived.

## What does not qualify

- Closed-source products with a thin open-source wrapper or SDK.
- Marketing repos (a README pointing at a paid product, with no runnable code).
- Coursework, abandoned demos, or empty scaffolds.

## How to suggest an entry

Open an issue or a pull request with:

1. The repository URL.
2. One sentence on what it does.
3. One sentence on the concrete job it does for an in-house legal team.

Keep descriptions factual. No superlatives, no vendor copy.
